# Noor Privacy Policy

Noor is an Islamic application that provides Quran, prayer times, reminders, and spiritual tools.

## Permissions

- Location permission: used to calculate local prayer times and Qibla direction.
- Notification permission: used for prayer alerts and Islamic reminders.

## Advertising

Noor may display advertisements through integrated advertising services. Advertising providers may process limited device information according to their own privacy policies.

## Data

Noor does not sell personal user data.
